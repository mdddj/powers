### 🚀 Quick Start

1. **Install a Power:**
   - Open Kiro IDE
   - Navigate to Powers Panel (👻⚡ icon)
   - Click "Add power from GitHub"
   - Paste the GitHub URL from above
   - Click "Install"

2. **Activate a Power:**
   - Simply mention relevant keywords in your conversation
   - Kiro will automatically activate the appropriate power
   - Or manually type the power name to force activation

3. **Use the Power:**
   - Follow the onboarding instructions
   - The power will guide you through its workflows
